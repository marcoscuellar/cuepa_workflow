Audit area

Policy & pricing

5findings in this area
F-01Refund rules contradict each other◆CriticalQuick win
Where/summer-circus-camp · camp pass product pages · /aftercareHeuristicH4 · Consistency and standardsEffortS

What an unused pass refunds, by page

Day pass · camp page75%
Day pass · checkout85%
Half-day · camp page0%
Half-day · checkout (typo)45%
  • Summer Camp page: unused day passes refunded “7 5%” (75%); half-day passes “nonrefundable”.
  • Day Pass and Weekly Pass product pages: “85% of the value of unused … passes is refunded automatically”.
  • Half-Day Pass product page: “4**5%” refunded, which contradicts “nonrefundable”.
  • After-school page states two rules: a full refund less a 20% fee 48 hours out, and full credit 30+ days out / 50% at 7–30 days.
Impact

Parents can quote whichever page favours them. This is the documented source of refund disputes months after a session.

Goal

One refund answer, no matter where a family reads it.

F-03Required family forms are not available on the site◆CriticalNew site
Where/services-2 (Forms and Policies) · site footerHeuristicH10 · Help and documentationEffortM
  • Forms page text indexed by Google: “This is a page with all CirquesExperiences waivers, permission slips, etc..” No documents found.
  • Footer has no waiver, refund, privacy or terms link; “Do Not Sell My Personal Information” is unlinked.
  • Paperwork review: liability waiver + photo release, medical/allergy/emergency form and approved-pickup list are missing.
Impact

No signed record exists when an injury, pickup question or photo complaint comes up. Chicago Public Schools sites also expect signed waivers and pickup lists.

Goal

Every family’s required forms signed and on file.

Sign-in and forms

How families get in, and how you know they’re ready

New families

Sign up while they pay

No account wall. They browse first, then add just an email at checkout. That becomes their family account.

Returning families

Sign in with an email link

No password to forget. Their kids, forms and past bookings are already there, so the next sign-up is faster.

For you

Forms status in your Room

Every child on a roster shows Forms ✓ or exactly what’s missing. Families get a reminder before forms expire for the new season.

Family database

Every family’s info in one place

One private database: parents, kids, emergency contacts, medical and allergy notes, signed forms, bookings and payments. The site, forms and your Room all read from it. Only your team can see it.

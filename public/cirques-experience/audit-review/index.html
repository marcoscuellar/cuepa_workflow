Chicago Public Schools

What still applies on CPS property.

Billing parents directly skips CPS vendor bidding, purchase orders and the $35K cap. Confirm current rules with CPS and your insurer.

Chicago · CPS property
01
Facility permit

Board Rule 6-25. Approved by the principal and building engineer. May include custodial fees or a school/PTO revenue split.

02
Staff background checks

Every coach on CPS grounds: FBI + Illinois State Police fingerprints, DCFS check, and PCC 2.0 training.

03
Insurance (COI)

$1M/$2M general liability plus Sexual Abuse & Molestation coverage, naming the Board of Education of the City of Chicago as additional insured.

04
Parent protection

Signed waivers, emergency medical consent and strict after-school pickup lists.
